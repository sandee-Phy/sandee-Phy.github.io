About Me

I am a doctoral research scholar in the Department of Physics at the Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur. My research focuses on Quantum Information Theory and the Foundations of Quantum Mechanics. I investigate quantum error-correcting codes, quantum entanglement, and mathematical frameworks governing non-classical systems.

Education

  • Ph.D. in Physics — Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur 2026 – Present
  • M.Sc. in Physics — Assam University, Silchar 2022 – 2024
  • B.Sc. in Physics (Honours) — G.C. College, Silchar 2018 – 2021

Research Interests

Quantum Information Theory

Quantum measurement theory, stabilizer generators, CSS codes, and fault-tolerant quantum computation schemes.

Foundations of Quantum Mechanics

Non-locality, contextuality, open quantum dynamics, and foundational mathematical physics.

Scientific Computing

Numerical simulation of stabilizer circuits, Lindbladian evolution, and entanglement measures using Python and Qiskit.
